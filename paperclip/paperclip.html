Drum contacts and 26-bit words

Amber: ten order tracks. Blue: sixteen address/control tracks. The highlighted row is the next word to execute. During Run, the simulator pauses before RIN A or RIN B so you can enter a value; click Run again to continue.

Syntax

Comments begin with //, ;, or #. Labels are assigned to original jump contacts 1–4. A label may be on its own line or before an instruction.
